# Vocabulary

## August 20, 2026

No new vocabulary was introduced or practiced in this session.

## 2026-09-08

### Needs reinforcement

- **plan to + verb** — use for intentions or planned actions.
  - Example: “I plan to take the English exam this week.”
- **prepare for + noun** — get ready for something.
  - Example: “I plan to prepare for the assessment this week.”
  - Avoid: “prepare to the assessment.”
- **be worried about + noun** — feel concerned about something.
  - Example: “I’m worried about the results.”
- **be confident (that) + clause** — feel sure about an ability or outcome.
  - Example: “I’m not confident I can reach B2 yet.”
- **mow the lawn** — cut the grass using a lawn mower.
- **topic** — natural word for what a conversation or discussion is about.

## 2026-09-09

### Needs reinforcement

- **be worried that + clause** — “I’m worried that I’ll get stuck on simple questions.”
- **get stuck on + noun** — “I might get stuck on a simple question.”
- **under pressure** — “It’s harder for me to remember words under pressure.”
- **go blank / my mind goes blank** — temporarily be unable to remember or think of what to say.
- **blank vs. blink** — blank = empty/unable to think; blink = briefly close and open the eyes.
- **If I had…, I would…** — hypothetical pattern.

## 2026-09-11

### Needs reinforcement

- **try to + base verb** — use **try to check / try to run**, not a mixed verb pattern.
  - Example: “I try to check logs and the database.”
  - Oral reminder: **try** = present; **tried** = past.
- **another + singular noun / other + plural noun**
  - “another system” / “other systems.”
- **start + -ing** — useful natural pattern for beginning an activity.
  - Example: “Then I start investigating.”
- **stay at/in a hotel** — natural hotel phrasing.
  - Example: “We stayed at a hotel in Sarnia.”
  - Avoid: “stay on the hotel.”
- **canal lock / lock** — a structure that raises or lowers boats between different water levels.
- **dream of + -ing** — express something you would really like to experience.
  - Example: “I dream of seeing a cruise ship.”
- **fall / in fall / in the fall** — the season is singular **fall**, not “falls.”
  - Example: “We like traveling in the fall.”
- **waterfall / waterfalls** — one waterfall; two or more waterfalls.
- **every season** — natural alternative to “every part of year.”
  - Example: “I enjoy traveling in every season.”
